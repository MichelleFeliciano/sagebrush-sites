How We'd Work Together

A simple, four-step process

No jargon, no surprise scope creep — just a clear path from idea to a live site.

1

Discover

A free intro call to understand your business, your booking platform, and what the site needs to actually do.

2

Design

A layout and style built around your brand — not a generic template — reviewed with you before any code is written.

3

Build

Hand-coded HTML/CSS/JS, fast-loading and mobile-first, with your booking platform linked or embedded.

4

Launch

Final review, go-live, and a walkthrough so you're not stuck if you need to update something yourself.

Pricing depends on scope — number of pages, gallery size, and booking integration all factor in. I'll always give you a clear quote before any work starts.

About Sagebrush Sites

Sagebrush Sites designs and builds websites for salons and beauty businesses — warm, mobile-first sites that load fast, show off the work through real photo galleries, and make booking an appointment effortless.

Sagebrush Sites is founded by Michelle Feliciano, based in Fort Worth, with a background in computer information systems and over a decade of client-focused experience. Web development is new territory specifically, but working closely with clients and getting the details right isn't — no bloated templates, no slow page loads, just sites that look as good as the work they represent and get browsers to actually book. Every project gets the same attention to detail as the concept work you see here.
